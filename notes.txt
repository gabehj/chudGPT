One of the things that we would do if we had more time would make the bot's messages relationally adaptive.
Instead of incrementing logits by a hyperparam every time, we could Instead have each word correspond to its 
own logit incrementor. Doing so, the bot could also become relationally adaptive, where after poor conversations,
it can disassociate certain messages with certain key words. Additionally, keywords could move between messages overtime 
if a message is successful when surrounded by others of a certain keyword.
